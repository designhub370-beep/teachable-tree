# Aroma Academy completion and redesign

## What will be built
- A free student signup screen shown before the academy website opens.
- A complete Academic Navy redesign with an education-first home page, large “Aroma Academy of Education” branding, study imagery, course grid, fee finder, offers, and study material.
- A new academy logo used across signup, navigation, and key pages.
- Finished teacher area for uploading PDFs, images, and tests and editing fees.
- Finished head area for adding, editing, and deleting classes, college courses, subjects, fees, offers, and materials.
- Mobile and desktop layouts with clear Hindi/Hinglish labels.

## Signup flow
- Students create a free account with name, phone number, email, and password.
- Existing students can sign in.
- The academy pages open after successful signup or sign-in.
- Teacher and Head keep their separate staff login.

## Visual direction
- Academic Navy palette: deep navy `#111C3B`, institutional blue `#234A72`, teal `#1C9A8B`, and warm yellow `#F2C14E`.
- Course-focused first page with a prominent study visual and immediate class/exam options.
- A stronger custom typography choice than the shown presets, selected to feel modern, trusted, and easy to read.
- Subtle notebook, textbook, formula, and classroom details in backgrounds without reducing readability.

## Technical details
- Student accounts use Lovable Cloud authentication; signup data remains protected.
- Existing classes, pricing, offers, and uploaded materials remain connected to Lovable Cloud.
- Uploaded study files use protected role-aware actions; public student downloads remain available as intended.
- Every page receives complete social and search metadata.
- Validate signup, sign-in, teacher/head controls, uploads, fees, navigation, and small-screen layout.
